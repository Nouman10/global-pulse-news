EDITORIAL STANDARD

Why there isn't a number lower than 20,000 here.

Every candidate topic is pulled from live Google Trends (geo=US and geo=GB) and current news feeds, then checked against a hard threshold: a verified 20,000+ monthly searches in the USA or UK, or a Google Trends item explicitly labelled 20K+, 50K+, 100K+ or higher. Anything labelled 100+, 200+, 500+ or 1,000+ is rejected. No estimates, no rounding up.

When a cycle finds nothing that clears the gate, we publish nothing. That is the point.
