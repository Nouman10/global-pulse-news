Hunger Games Prequel Draws 2M Searches

A new prequel, a divided press and a solid opening weekend. Separating the review noise from the numbers.

By Global Pulse Desk22 November 20264 min read
Gate passed: "hunger games sunrise on the reaping" — 2,000,000 US + 200,000 GB searches, Google Trends weekly data, checked 22 Nov 2026. Minimum: 5,000.

Quick Key Takeaways (TL;DR)

  • The story: Sunrise on the Reaping is a prequel following a young Haymitch Abernathy, with Joseph Zada and Woody Harrelson.
  • The split: The Guardian called it "surreally awful"; Empire said "enjoyable enough"; the BBC reported opinion sharply divided.
  • The numbers: $21 million in previews (Variety) and a $76 million global running cume (Deadline).
  • The demand: 2 million US searches and 200,000 UK searches in Google Trends weekly data — one of the strongest entertainment queries of the week.

What the film is

The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping returns to Panem decades before Katniss Everdeen's story, centring on a young Haymitch Abernathy. Joseph Zada plays Haymitch, with Woody Harrelson among the cast. Time Magazine published a guide explaining how the prequel connects to the original trilogy, and the BBC reported that new cast members described joining the franchise as "nerve-racking."

Why audiences are searching

Google Trends weekly data records 2,000,000 US searches and 200,000 UK searches for the title in the week of 22 November 2026. Related queries include cast names, runtime, and where the film was filmed — the familiar pattern of a release people are deciding whether to see.

Critics are genuinely split

The Guardian's review called the film a "surreally awful prequel to the Katniss Everdeen era." NPR's verdict was that it "feels like sunset for the 'Hunger Games' franchise." The New York Times titled its review "Another Tribute," and Vulture said the film "tries a vibe shift." On the other side, Empire called it "enjoyable enough," and Forbes reported that it set a Rotten Tomatoes audience-score record. The BBC explicitly framed the response as a split.

When press and audience disagree

A gap between critic scores and audience scores is not unusual for a franchise with a dedicated fanbase. For readers, the practical point is that the polarized coverage is itself a poor predictor of whether you will enjoy it; the same film is being described as both a low point and a record-setter within the same week.

How it opened

Variety reported $21 million in preview earnings. Deadline reported a $76 million running global cume as of 20 November 2026. Those are strong opening numbers for a prequel, and they help explain why the title dominates search interest across both the US and UK.
