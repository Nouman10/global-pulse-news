The short list

Weekend guides are useful precisely because they make a decision for you. TVLine's 22 November "What To Watch Sunday" roundup put Tracker — with Josh Holloway — at the top of its list, alongside a Hallmark movie featuring Rachael Leigh Cook. That is a reasonable starting pair: one ongoing series and one self-contained film.

The free-streaming angle

Collider reported the same day that classic television is getting a second life on free streaming platforms. For anyone tired of subscription hopping, that is the most useful tip of the week. Availability is regional, so check your local catalogue before committing.

If you are in the UK

UK listings looked different. The Guardian's "TV tonight" column pointed to a spooky Welsh drama starring Michael Socha, while The Telegraph's weekend guide covered The Battle for Blackpool and Honey. The BBC also carried a widely shared moment from a live TV interview involving Argentina midfielder Leandro Paredes — a reminder that "trending TV" is not always a scripted show.
